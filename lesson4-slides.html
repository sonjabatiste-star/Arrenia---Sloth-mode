🌴 Mission: Command the Decimals

This week you'll compare and order decimals (even negative ones), then multiply and divide them with speed and precision.

Worked Example 1

Order Rational Numbers, Including Negatives

Order least to greatest: −3.2, 1.05, −3.08, 0.9

Negatives first — more negative is smaller: −3.2 is less than −3.08 (it's farther left on the line).
Ordered: −3.2, −3.08, 0.9, 1.05
🦥 Ask Arrenia: Why is −3.2 less than −3.08, even though 3.2 > 3.08 without the negative signs?
Concept

Multiplying Decimals

Multiply as if there were no decimal points, then count total decimal places in both factors to place the point in your answer.

3.4 × 0.6
34 × 6 = 204
3.4 has 1 decimal place, 0.6 has 1 decimal place → 2 total
3.4 × 0.6 = 2.04
🦥 Ask Arrenia: Estimate 3.4 × 0.6 by rounding first (3 × 0.6 = 1.8) — does 2.04 seem reasonable?
Worked Example 2

Dividing Decimals

To divide by a decimal, shift both decimal points right until the divisor is a whole number.

7.5 ÷ 0.25
Shift both 2 places: 750 ÷ 25
= 30
🦥 Ask Arrenia: Why can we shift both decimal points the same number of places without changing the answer?
Challenge

Multi-Step Decimal Problem

Orange juice costs $3.75 per gallon. You buy 2.4 gallons and pay with a $10 bill. How much change do you get?

3.75 × 2.4 = 9.00
10.00 − 9.00 = $1.00 change
🦥 Ask Arrenia: How could you estimate this problem first to check that $1.00 makes sense?
