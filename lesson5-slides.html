🦥 Mission: Explore Below Zero

Numbers don't stop at zero — this week you'll work with negative numbers and learn to measure how far any number sits from zero using absolute value.

🦥 Ask Arrenia: If a sloth climbs 3 feet up a tree and then a submarine sinks 3 feet below sea level, how are those two "3s" different?
Concept

Negative Numbers on the Number Line

Negative numbers extend the number line to the left of zero. They represent quantities "opposite" of positive — below sea level, below zero degrees, or money owed.

-10 0 10
🦥 Ask Arrenia: Which is smaller: −8 or −2? Why might that feel backwards at first?
Concept

Absolute Value = Distance from Zero

Absolute value tells you how far a number is from zero — always a non-negative distance, no matter which direction.

|−7| = 7   (7 units from zero)
|7| = 7   (also 7 units from zero)
Same distance, opposite directions — same absolute value.
🦥 Ask Arrenia: Can absolute value ever be negative? Why or why not?
Worked Example 1

Comparing with Absolute Value

Which number is farther from zero: −12 or 9?

|−12| = 12
|9| = 9
Since 12 > 9, −12 is farther from zero, even though it's the smaller number.
🦥 Ask Arrenia: Is it possible for a number to be smaller than another but have a bigger absolute value? Give an example.
Worked Example 2

Real-World Contexts

A submarine is at an elevation of −120 feet (120 feet below sea level). A diver is at −45 feet. Who is deeper?

|−120| = 120    |−45| = 45
120 > 45, so the submarine is deeper, even though −120 < −45.
🦥 Ask Arrenia: What other real-life situations use negative numbers besides depth, temperature, and money?
Challenge

Combining Absolute Values

A hiker starts at an elevation of 0 ft, climbs to +85 ft, then a canyon trail dips to −30 ft. What is the total distance traveled (up plus back down), using absolute value?

|85| + |−30 − 85| = 85 + 115 = 200 feet total distance
🦥 Ask Arrenia: Why does "total distance traveled" use absolute value instead of just adding the elevations directly?
